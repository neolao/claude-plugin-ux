- **Scope:** full UI (`src/`)
- **Captures:** none — app could not be launched
- **Agents run:** review-states, review-accessibility, review-content, review-consistency, review-visual

## Summary

Findings: 3 (High: 2, Medium: 1, Low: 0)

The operator's daily jobs are slowed by where the controls live and by what happens after an archive; one error state showed developers' text to the team.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Axis | Where | Issue | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | High | flows | `src/boards/BoardArchived.tsx` | After "Archive board" the operator lands on a page with no navigation, no link and no undo | Stay on the Boards list and offer undo from the toast |
| F2 | High | content | `src/boards/BoardDetail.tsx` | The error state showed the exception text (`GET /boards/42 → 500`) | Show a plain message with a retry |
| F3 | Medium | flows | `src/boards/BoardDetail.tsx` | Renaming a board is only reachable through Settings › Boards, while the job arises on the board | Rename inline from the board's title |

## Suggested next designs

1. `/ux:design F1` — the operator ends the archive job where the next board is
2. `/ux:design F3` — one click fewer per rename, several times a day
