# Product — Taskboard

## What it is

Taskboard is a web tool for small teams to organize their work on shared boards.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard; laptop screens from 1280 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team member | regular | daily | at the desk, several members on the same board at the same time | keep the board current |

## Constraints

- **Performance / offline / other:** online only; the API answers under 500 ms
- **Accessibility target:** none stated
- **Localization:** English only
