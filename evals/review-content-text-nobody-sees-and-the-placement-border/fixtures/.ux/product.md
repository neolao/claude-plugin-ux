# Product — Rolodex

## What it is

Rolodex is a web contact manager for small associations: volunteers keep
the member list up to date and import sign-up sheets.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard, touch on tablets; from 768 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volunteer | occasional | monthly | at home, after a members' day | keep the member list current |

## Constraints

- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Localization:** English and French

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | One person in the list | Member, Entry |
| Import | Bringing a CSV sheet into the list | Upload |
