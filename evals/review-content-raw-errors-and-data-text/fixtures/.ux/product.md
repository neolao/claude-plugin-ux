# Product — Rolodex

## What it is

Rolodex is a desktop contact manager for small associations: volunteers keep
the member list up to date and import sign-up sheets exported from a
spreadsheet.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** desktop (Electron, macOS and Windows)
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard; laptops from 1280 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volunteer | occasional | once or twice a year | at home, after a members' day | bring the sign-up sheet into the list without retyping it |

## Constraints

- **Accessibility target:** none stated
- **Localization:** English and French

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | One person in the list | Member, Entry |
| Import | Bringing a CSV sheet into the list | Upload |
