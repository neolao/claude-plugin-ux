# Product — Ledger

## What it is

Ledger is a web bookkeeping tool for one-person companies: the bookkeeper
enters invoices and marks them paid.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard; desktop screens from 1280 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bookkeeper | expert | weekly | at the office desk, wired network, one browser window | keep the invoices of the company file current |

Each company file belongs to one bookkeeper; nobody else opens it.

## Constraints

- **Performance / offline / other:** online only, no offline use; the API answers under 500 ms
- **Accessibility target:** none stated
- **Localization:** English only
