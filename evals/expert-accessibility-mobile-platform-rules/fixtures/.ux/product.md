# Product

Fieldlog — a mobile app where field technicians log the inspections they carry out on site.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** mobile (iOS and Android)
- **Devices & input:** touch on a phone, one-handed, often outdoors; no external keyboard
- **Runs with:** `npx expo start`

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technician | occasional | daily, a few minutes per site | standing, bright light, sometimes gloves | record an inspection and move to the next site |

## Constraints

- **Brand / design system:** none
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA, VoiceOver and TalkBack supported
- **Localization:** English, French
- **Performance / offline / other:** works offline, syncs later
