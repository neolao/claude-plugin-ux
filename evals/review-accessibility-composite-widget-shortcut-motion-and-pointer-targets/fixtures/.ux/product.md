# Product — Planner

## What it is

Planner is a web tool for logistics coordinators: they place deliveries on a day grid and follow the live position of the vans.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard only; desktop screens from 1600 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coordinator | expert | daily | office desk, one large monitor | keep every delivery placed and every van tracked |

## Constraints

- **Brand / design system:** none
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Localization:** English only
- **Performance / offline / other:** online only
