# Product

Shelfmate — the web back office of a chain of bookshops: clerks reorder titles, track deliveries from suppliers and keep the shop's stock right.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** desktop browser at the counter, mouse + keyboard
- **Runs with:** `npm run dev`

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shop clerk | occasional | a few times a week | at the counter, between customers | keep the shelves stocked without thinking about the tool |
| Chain buyer | expert | daily | head office, two screens | place and follow supplier orders for every shop |

## Constraints

- **Brand / design system:** none
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Localization:** English only
- **Performance / offline / other:** none

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Title | A book as sold, identified by its ISBN | Book, Product, Item |
| Delivery | A parcel of titles sent by a supplier to one shop | Shipment, Order, Parcel |
| Shop | One physical bookshop of the chain | Store, Branch |
