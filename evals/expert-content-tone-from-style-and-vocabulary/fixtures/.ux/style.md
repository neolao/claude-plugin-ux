# Look and feel — Shelfmate

## Intent

A quiet ledger that stays out of the clerk's way: calm, precise, plain.
**Not this:** the playful tone of consumer shopping apps — the clerk has a customer waiting.

## Tone of content

Terse and precise. Imperative verbs, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no first person ("we", "our"). A message names the thing and the next step, nothing else.
