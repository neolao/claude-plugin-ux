# Product

Ledgerly — a web app where freelancers write invoices and follow their payments.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard, laptop and desktop screens (1280 px and up); no touch use observed
- **Runs with:** `npm run dev`

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freelancer | occasional | weekly | at a desk, a few minutes at a time | send an invoice and see who has paid |

## Constraints

- **Brand / design system:** none
- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Localization:** English and French
- **Performance / offline / other:** light and dark theme follow the OS setting
