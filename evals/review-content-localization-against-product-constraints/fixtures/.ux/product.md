# Product — Deskmap

## What it is

Deskmap is a web app for booking meeting rooms from an office floor plan.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** web
- **Devices & input:** mouse + keyboard; desktop browsers from 1024 px wide

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Office employee | occasional | a few times a month | at a desk, a meeting about to start | reserve a free room without asking reception |

## Constraints

- **Accessibility target:** WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Localization:** English and Arabic (right-to-left); Arabic strings run about 20 % longer

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Room | A bookable meeting room | Space |
| Slot | A free period of a room | Time, Window |
| Booking | A room reserved for a slot | Reservation |
