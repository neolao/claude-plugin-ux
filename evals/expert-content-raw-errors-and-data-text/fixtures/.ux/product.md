# Product

Cotiz — a desktop application for small associations: the volunteers keep the member list, record dues and send reminders.

## Platform & surface

- **Platform:** desktop (Electron), macOS and Windows
- **Devices & input:** laptops, mouse + keyboard
- **Runs with:** `npm run start`

## Users

| Role | Expertise | Frequency of use | Context of use | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volunteer | occasional | once or twice a month, more after an event | at home, in the evening | keep the member list right without a manual |
| Treasurer | occasional | weekly | at home | know who has paid |

## Constraints

- **Brand / design system:** none
- **Accessibility target:** platform guidelines
- **Localization:** English and French
- **Performance / offline / other:** works offline, syncs when online

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | A person in the association's list, member or not | Person, Record, Entry |
| Member list | All contacts of the association | Directory, Database |
| Dues | The yearly membership fee | Subscription, Fee |
